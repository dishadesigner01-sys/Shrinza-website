Description

Every piece is hand-finished to order in our Delhi atelier, so please allow 7–10 business days before dispatch. Shipping is complimentary across India via tracked courier. For international delivery or a rush timeline, consult the studio on WhatsApp and we'll confirm what's possible before you order.

Made in India. Every SHRINZA garment is designed and hand-finished in our atelier in Delhi.

Cut, embroidered, and finished by hand in our Delhi workshop, then quality-checked piece by piece. Each garment is wrapped in acid-free tissue and packed in a branded SHRINZA box to protect embellishment and embroidery in transit.

Made-to-order and bridal pieces are non-returnable once production has begun. Ready-to-ship items may be exchanged within 3 days of delivery if unworn, unaltered, and in original packaging, subject to inspection. Colours may vary slightly from photos due to screen settings and hand-dyeing. For sizing questions or custom alterations, please consult the studio before you order.
